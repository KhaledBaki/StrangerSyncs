02 / MISSION

Why StrangerSyncs?

Forgetting names and personal details can make everyday social interactions harder. StrangerSyncs explores a technological way to support memory during those moments.

24,000+

People living with dementia in the Ottawa region

Ottawa Public Health, Champlain health region data.

The problem

Ottawa Public Health puts the number of people living with dementia in the Champlain region at over 24,000, and it's rising as our population ages. Long before any diagnosis, though, the same kind of memory friction shows up in everyday life — a name that won't surface, a face that feels familiar but unplaceable, a connection that quietly slips away.

Our project focuses on one simple question: what if technology could provide a small second memory? StrangerSyncs isn't a medical device — it's a wearable aid for the moment recognition fails, built around a real and growing local need.
